Help centre

How can we help?

Answers to common questions, plus a direct line to the Solo Soul team.

No matches. Email us and we will help directly.

Can I change the date of my booking?

Yes, subject to availability and at least 48 hours before the experience. Open the booking in My Solo Soul and contact support with the new date.

How do refunds work?

Paid bookings can request a refund from My Solo Soul → Payments. We review every request, then Paystack processes approved refunds to the original payment method.

Where is my ticket?

After payment, your ticket is available in My Solo Soul and emailed to your registered address. Each ticket has a reference and QR code.

My payment failed — what now?

No booking is confirmed until payment succeeds. Open the booking and use the payment retry option, or contact support and we will send a new payment link.

Do I have to come with someone?

No. Solo Soul experiences are designed for people arriving alone. Hosts introduce the group gently, and there is no pressure to perform.

Can you accommodate dietary or access needs?

Yes. Add the detail when booking, or raise a support ticket and we will confirm with the venue before the event.

Still stuck?

Email SSoulZAR@outlook.com or send a ticket. We reply within one business day.
