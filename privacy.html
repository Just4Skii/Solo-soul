Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026 · Last updated 2 November 2026

Solo Soul Experiences (“we”, “us”) curates and hosts solo experiences in Durban, KwaZulu-Natal. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We process personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and, if you provide it, your mobile number.
  • Booking details: the experience, date, number of guests, notes you add and the status of your booking.
  • Payment records: a payment reference, amount and status. Card details are entered on the payment provider’s page and are not stored by us.
  • Technical data: your browser stores a session so you stay signed in. We do not run advertising trackers.

Why we use it

  • To confirm and manage your booking, issue your entry ticket and respond to enquiries.
  • To process payments and prevent duplicate or fraudulent bookings.
  • To send essential service messages about an experience you booked.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Who processes it

We use service providers for hosting, database storage, email and payments. They process data on our instructions and under their own security commitments. Payment card data is handled by our payment provider, not by us.

How long we keep it

Booking and payment records are kept for as long as required for accounting and legal purposes. Account details are kept until you ask us to delete them, unless we must retain a record by law.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or object to processing. Email SSoulZAR@outlook.com and we will respond within a reasonable period. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Security

Access to booking data is restricted, admin accounts are password-protected, and data is transmitted over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your information we will notify you and the Regulator as required.

Children

Our experiences are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update this page and its effective date.
